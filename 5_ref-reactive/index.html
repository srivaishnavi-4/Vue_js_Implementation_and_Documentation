What you are learning

ref(): searchText, selectedFilter, tasks and nextId are refs. In JavaScript, read or update them using .value.

reactive(): taskForm groups title, description, priority and due date. Access properties directly, such as taskForm.title.

Deep reactivity: Changing task.completed updates the task display and dashboard counts automatically.

computed(): filteredTasks, activeCount and completedCount recalculate when their reactive dependencies change.
